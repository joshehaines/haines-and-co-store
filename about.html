Our Story

Passionate about running, just like you.

Haines & Co. was founded in 2018 by a group of passionate runners who knew there had to be a better way to shop for running gear. We were frustrated by generic sports retailers who treated running like any other sport. We believed runners needed specialists — people who understand the difference between a tempo run and an easy recovery day, who know why shoes matter, and who live and breathe this sport.

Today, we work with the world's leading running brands to bring you the products we genuinely use and believe in. Every shoe on our shelves has been tested. Every piece of clothing has been worn by someone on our team. We're not just selling products; we're sharing our passion for running with ours.

Whether you're training for a marathon, getting back into running, or simply looking to upgrade your kit, we're here to help. Our team of experienced runners is ready to offer advice, recommend products, and support your running journey.
